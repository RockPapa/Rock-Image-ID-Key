# Tree ID characteristic master list

Working consolidation of:

- `[OLD]` — species already represented in the old `TreeId.html`
- `[BARTRAM]` — additions from `Bartram TREE species not yet in TreeId.txt`

## How this list is organized

- For a **simple leaf**, toothless/toothed/lobed describes the whole blade.
- For a **compound leaf**, it describes the individual leaflets.
- A lobed leaf may also be toothed. For a beginner-facing key, **lobed takes precedence**.
- `†` means the species is intentionally cross-listed because its leaves or leaflets vary.
- `‡` means the species may also have whorled leaves and needs an explanatory note in the app.
- Historical Bartram names are retained in parentheses when useful; the currently accepted or commonly used name is placed first.
