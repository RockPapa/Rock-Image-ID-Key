Southeast U.S. Unique Plant Habitats

Six habitats where geology leaves a recognizable botanical diagnostic. These are typical assemblages for comparison—not exhaustive property checklists and not claims that every listed plant requires the named rock.

DiagnosticUnusually informative for this substrate or community.
CharacteristicRegularly favored, but also found in other habitats.
CompanionCommon in the setting but weak as a geological indicator.
Interpretation rule: infer habitat from a plant assemblage plus landform, exposed rock and soil—not from one species. “Mafic” and “base-rich” overlap but are not synonyms; ultramafic soils can be chemically stressful despite their magnesium richness.
